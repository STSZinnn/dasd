# Sovereign SMP v1.7.5 — The Complete Seven

Adds **Artifact VI — Stormvault** and **Artifact VII — Hexcaller** to the existing five artifacts. The Sovereign Mace remains a separate special weapon.

- Stormvault: Unbreakable copper spear with Sharpness V, Lunge I and Looting III. Airborne strikes build Wind Momentum; Gale Burst (right-click) launches a wind projectile; Updraft (sneak-right-click) grants a controllable aerial burst; Eye of the Tempest (F) makes a moving tornado with bounded pulses and a finishing gust.
- Hexcaller: Unbreakable Blaze Rod with Sharpness III, Looting III and custom staff visuals. Attacks build Hex Marks; Fangs of Judgment (right-click) creates evoker-fang strikes and a Hexbound ring; Vexbound (sneak-right-click) summons two temporary Vexes; Illager's Dominion (F) makes three telegraphed ritual waves with Vex support.
- Both artifacts have unique UUID tokens, owner persistence in the existing `artifacts.yml`, claim-on-pickup, death unclaim/drop, mint/recover commands, ability HUD and OP-only config pages.
- New automatically styled gradient item lore, animated tooltip backgrounds, and 16×16 custom item models. The **combined** FrostPVP + Sovereign resource pack keeps all previous assets.
- Original Echo Scythe, Witherborn, Tidelord, Dragonfang, Paleheart and Sovereign Mace preserved.
